viberadio

Your agent’s output as audio — narration, podcast, or music.

npx viberadio-fm

What it does

viberadio listens to your coding agent so you don’t have to stare at it. It turns the agent’s output into audio you can take in away from the screen — as a narrated summary, a podcast-style episode, or music.

  • Three output modes: narration, podcast, or music
  • Review what your agent did while you walk, drive, or cook
  • Runs locally next to your agent — your machine, your keys
  • Installs and starts with a single npx command
