Brand guide

The identity and interface rules behind getvibe.dev.

A quiet signal board for local-first companions. Direct language, exact commands, and no SaaS theater.

Identity

getvibe.dev is the catalog for The Vibe Suite. It helps developers choose a companion for an agent coding workflow.

The catalog has no account, paid plan, or checkout. Each tool page must describe its own network and credential behavior accurately.

Mark and wordmark

The pulse represents a small companion that stays active beside the coding agent. Keep the path, round caps, and amber signal color intact.
