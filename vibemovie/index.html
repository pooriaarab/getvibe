vibemovie

Your coding session as a short recap video.

npx vibemovie

What it does

vibemovie cuts your coding session down to a short recap video. Instead of scrolling back through a wall of agent output, you get a watchable summary of what happened — and something you can hand to people who weren’t there.

  • Turns a finished session into a short recap video
  • Compresses a full session of agent scrollback into a quick watch
  • Generated locally from your own session
  • Installs and starts with a single npx command
